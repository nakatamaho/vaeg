HFSHOW - 640x400 full-colour pictures on the HyPER-FRAME+ (vaeg M149)

V3 mode, MS-DOS 4.0, PC-88VA with a Digital Arts HyPER-FRAME+.

  HFSHOW            send the first *.RGB picture of the current
                    directory to the board, wait for a key, then stop
                    the board (the main screen returns)
  HFSHOW /STAY      leave the board displaying when the program ends
  HFSHOW file       another 640x400x24 picture (R G B bytes, 768000)

The pictures are synthetic, 640x400, drawn for a 4:3 monitor:
TETRA.RGB (raytrace.py, on hfshow-msdos4.d88): a mirror sphere resting
on red, green and blue spheres at the vertices of a regular
tetrahedron, over a checkerboard tile floor.
MANDEL.RGB (mandel.py, on hfmandel-msdos4.d88): a Mandelbrot-set
close-up of Seahorse valley, smooth colouring. Everything goes through I/O ports D0, D3-D8, DA, DC and EE
only.

On the real board the picture appears on the monitor connected to the
board's output only once its main RGB input (CN2) is connected; see
docs/agents/tasks/M149. In vaeg, enable Device - HyPER-FRAME+... - and
pick "HyperFrame+ ON" (full screen) or "右上に出力" (top right).
