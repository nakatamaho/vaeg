VASHOW - 640x200 65536-colour pictures on the PC-88VA (vaeg M149)

V3 mode, MS-DOS 4.0, any PC-88VA / VA2 / VA3. No extra board.

  VASHOW            show the first *.G16 picture of the current
                    directory; a key restores the screen
  VASHOW file       a given picture (640x200 words, G6 R5 B5,
                    little endian, rows top to bottom, 256000 bytes)

The pictures are synthetic, rendered at 640x200 for a 4:3 monitor and
reduced to 65536 colours with a 4x4 ordered dither. The first two are
the scenes of the HyPER-FRAME+ sample disks:
TETRA.G16 (on va65536-tetra-msdos4.d88): a mirror sphere resting on
red, green and blue spheres at the vertices of a regular tetrahedron,
over a checkerboard tile floor.
MANDEL.G16 (on va65536-mandel-msdos4.d88): a Mandelbrot-set close-up
of Seahorse valley, smooth colouring.
METABALL.G16 (on va65536-metaball-msdos4.d88): three metaballs, each
with the field (1 - r^2/R^2)^3, yellow, cyan and magenta, flowing into
one another above the same checkerboard floor; HSV colouring, the hues
blending across the necks.

Sources: demos/native65536 and demos/hyperframe+ of vaeg
(https://github.com/nakatamaho/vaeg). 2-clause BSD licence.
