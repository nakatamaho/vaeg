PC-8801/8001 and PCG-8100/8800 emulation references

vaeg's PC-8801-side behaviour (V1/V2 mode, the PC-8001 machines) and its
PCG-8100/8800 emulation were written with reference to two PC-8801
emulators. No ROM or other file of either is included.

X88000 1.5.3
  Author:  Manuke (written 1998-2018; PCG-8800 support assisted by apaslothy)
  Site:    https://quagma.sakura.ne.jp/manuke/x88000.html
  Status:  public domain (PDS), as stated in its X88000Src.txt: no
           permission or acknowledgement is required. Acknowledged here.
  Use:     behavioural reference; some routines re-implemented in C from
           its source (io/pcg88.c from PC88Pcg.cpp; port and display details
           listed in docs/agents/DECISIONS/ADR-0016-v1v2-mode-series.md,
           "Derivations").

QUASI88 0.7.4
  Author:  showtime
  Site:    https://www.eonet.ne.jp/~showtime/quasi88/
  Status:  copyright of its author; its core under terms equivalent to the
           modified BSD license, its sound output under the MAME/XMAME and
           fmgen terms.
  Use:     behavioural reference only (PCG-8100 write timing, the 8253 at
           3.9936 MHz and its counter modes); no QUASI88 code is used.
