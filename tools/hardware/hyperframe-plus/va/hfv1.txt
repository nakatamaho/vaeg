HyPER-FRAME+ test pattern on the PC-88VA (vaeg M149, HFV1)

V3 mode, MS-DOS 4.0. Writes A:\HFLOG.TXT. If the photograph and the
log disagree, the log is right.

HFV1            read the board ports (PRE line), run the JPGVHF
                viewer's initialisation without its PC-98 BIOS call
                (EE=C0, D0=A5, D3=00, 8 points at Y=0191, D0=A1,
                32 points at Y=0193, D0=E1), draw 640x400 (eight
                colour bars on y 0..299, a grey ramp on y 300..399),
                and read 12 pixels back (RB lines). Leave the board
                displaying; photograph the HF+ monitor after it ends.
HFV1 /NOINIT    skip the initialisation (draw only).
HFV1 /WORD      select coordinates with word OUTs (D6, D4), as the
                viewer does, instead of bytes in the paper order
                (D7, D6, D5, D4).
HFV1 /OFF       write the viewer's stop sequence D0=00, EE=00.

RB lines show expected vs. read-back RGB. MISMATCH only means the
read path differs; look at the monitor regardless. The D8/DA/DC
values in PRE before the pattern show what the board drives.

Exit codes: 0 read-back matched (or /OFF), 1 option, 2 machine,
4 log not written, 11h a pixel differed.

Everything is drawn through I/O ports D0, D3-D8, DA, DC, EE only.
Nothing touches EC, port 1D0h, or any memory window.
